\begin{frame}
      \frametitle{Conclusions}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Deploying only \glspl{MMR} has the largest feed uranium 
              \gls{SWU} requirements
        \item Deploying primarily VOYGRs has the largest enriched uranium 
              requriements
        \item Delaying the transition time and increasing the \glspl{LWR}
              lifetime decreases material requirements
        \item Increasing the Xe-100 and \gls{MMR} buildshare increases the 
              \gls{HALEU} 
        \item Increasing the \gls{MMR} build share increases all metrics
              because more VOYGRs are deployed
        \item Increasing the VOYGR build share has opposite 
              effects as increasing the Xe-100 buildshare
      \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
